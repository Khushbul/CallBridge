Callbreak Rules & 20 Rounds

1. Deck & Trump: Played with 52 cards (no jokers). Spades (♠) is the permanent trump suit.

2. Dealing & Bidding: 13 cards are dealt to each of 4 players. Players bid (call) 1 to 8+ tricks.

3. Strict Trick Play: Players MUST follow the suit led. If they hold a higher card of the led suit, they MUST play it. If void in the led suit, they MUST play a Spade (trump) if they have one.

4. Scoring: Making the bid awards the bid value + 0.1 for every extra trick (e.g. bid 3, won 4 = 3.1). Failing the bid awards negative points (-bid).

5. 20-Round Championship: Matches run for 5, 10, 15, or up to 20 rounds. The player with the highest total score wins the tournament podium!
